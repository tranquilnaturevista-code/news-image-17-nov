One image summing up the world's news of Tuesday 17 November 2026:
the U.S. strikes on Iran and the Strait of Hormuz tanker attacks,
Ukraine's strike on the Kerch Bridge, the Trump–Xi–Putin summit in
Beijing, the United States losing its measles elimination status,
and Japan scrambling jets after a Russian airspace incursion.

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) copies `entry.png` to
`/output/entry.png`. No network is used; both runs write the same bytes.
